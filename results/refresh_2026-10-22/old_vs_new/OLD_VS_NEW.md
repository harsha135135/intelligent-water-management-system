# Old models vs retrained, on the refresh study's dates

Generated by `python -m src.models.old_vs_new` (re-render with `--report`) from the CSVs in this folder; nothing below is typed by hand.

The published study's models were fitted on data up to **24 Mar 2026**. The refresh study refitted them up to **22 Sep 2026**. Here the March-fitted models, loaded unchanged from `results/chronos2/`, forecast exactly the refresh study's origins, tanks and hours, and are scored on the same rows as the retrained ones.

## In short

- **Chronos-2, TimesFM, Timestral:** nothing was trained, so there is nothing to compare; their forecasts for these dates are the same whichever study they come from.
- **NPTS, ETS, Theta, DynamicOptimizedTheta, SeasonalNaive:** identical forecasts on every row.
- **PatchTST:** on the benchmark grid the March-fitted network beats the retrained one at 6 of 6 horizons, by a margin whose 95 % interval excludes zero at 6. These are one training run each, and the bootstrap resamples origins, not training seeds: it shows that this retrained network is worse than the March one, not that retraining makes the model worse in general (see the training-data note in §1).
- **PatchTST-Tuned:** on the benchmark grid the March-fitted network beats the retrained one at 6 of 6 horizons, by a margin whose 95 % interval excludes zero at 6. These are one training run each, and the bootstrap resamples origins, not training seeds: it shows that this retrained network is worse than the March one, not that retraining makes the model worse in general (see the training-data note in §1).
- **Holdout PatchTST (45-day view):** daily campus MAE 145.4 KL March-fitted vs 130.3 KL retrained, hourly MAE 0.302 vs 0.312 KL/h; both under-forecast campus demand by more than half (volume bias -64 % and -57 %), so neither is usable for the daily total.
- **Calibration:** re-fitting it on 9 Jul 2026 – 6 Sep 2026 changed the daily MAE by NPTS -3.23, Timestral-209M-ZS -2.41, TimesFM2.5-ZS -0.65, Chronos2-ZS -0.02, SeasonalNaive-24 +0.40 KL/day, and moved the daily band's coverage towards its nominal 80 % for 4 of 5 models (§3).
- *Interpretation:* none of the calibrated models gains from a retrain (zero-shot, or refitted at every forecast); what fresher data can improve for them is the calibration. On these dates re-fitting it clearly helped NPTS and Timestral-209M-ZS (daily MAE down by 1 KL or more). It was a wash on daily MAE for TimesFM2.5-ZS, Chronos2-ZS, SeasonalNaive-24, and volume bias moved closer to zero only for NPTS, Timestral-209M-ZS.

## What can change, and what cannot

| Model | Fitted to this campus | Re-run here |
|---|---|---|
| Chronos-2, TimesFM 2.5, Timestral | nothing (zero-shot) | no: the same history gives the same forecast, so old and new are one forecast; only their **calibration** can differ (§3) |
| NPTS, ETS, Theta, DynamicOptimizedTheta, SeasonalNaive | nothing that persists: refitted per tank on the history handed to them at forecast time | yes, as a check that old = new |
| PatchTST, PatchTST-Tuned | network weights | yes: the real old-vs-retrained test (§1, §2) |
| Calibration (volume factors, conformal offsets, daily band) | per model and tank | yes: fitted 8 Jan 2026 – 8 Mar 2026 vs 9 Jul 2026 – 6 Sep 2026 (§3) |

## Caveats

- **The March models were fitted on Waltr's old figures.** Waltr has since revised its history (flows zeroed in hours where the level moved; campus outflow on the published period fell 4.8 %), and every row here is scored on the revised figures. Part of any old-vs-new gap is that revision, not the six extra months of data.
- **Calendar covariates are blank after 30 May 2026 for both.** PatchTST reads them; neither the old nor the retrained network sees the 2026-27 semester calendar.
- The holdout PatchTST and the calibration are each one fit; there is no interval on §2 and §3, only on §1.
